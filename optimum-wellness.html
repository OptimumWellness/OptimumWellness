Scope of Service & Professional Disclaimer

1. No Financial Advice or Services: Although the founder of Optimum Wellness holds the credential of Registered Life Planner (RLP), this business does not operate as a financial adviser, wealth manager, or fiduciary. No part of the services, discussions, interactive tools, or materials provided through this program constitutes financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. You should consult a regulated Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) professional for any financial decisions.

2. Fitness and Nutrition Scope of Practice: All fitness coaching, exercise programming, and nutritional guidance are delivered in a qualified capacity as a UK Level 3 Personal Trainer and Level 4 Nutrition coach.

  • Nutritional Guidance: We provide nutritional advice, education, and dietary planning designed to support general health, fitness, and lifestyle goals.
  • Not Medical Advice: We are not Registered Dietitians, Medical Doctors, or Registered Physiotherapists. Our services do not include the medical diagnosis, treatment, or management of any physical injuries, clinical eating disorders, or medical conditions (such as diabetes, renal disease, or gastrointestinal disorders).

3. Client Responsibility and Medical Clearance: You acknowledge that participating in exercise and altering your diet carries inherent risks. It is your responsibility to ensure you are in good physical health and, where appropriate, to seek medical clearance from your GP before starting any new exercise or nutritional regimen. You agree to disclose all pre-existing medical conditions, injuries, or relevant health histories prior to commencing coaching.
